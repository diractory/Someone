Put your own songs here (mp3, m4a, ogg), only songs you own or are allowed to use.
Then in the app: Settings -> Music library, add a line like:
  Calm Piano | calm | music/calm-piano.mp3
Moods you can use: sad, calm, comfort, happy, upbeat, romantic, study, sleep.
Spotify or YouTube links also work (they open in the app):
  My Sad Playlist | sad | https://open.spotify.com/playlist/XXXX
